Dates are interpreted from DDMM: 25 September–31 December are in 2026; 1 January–24 September are in 2027. September 2027 includes AFL Grand Final Friday with its date TBC; the September 2026 occurrence has been removed. Holiday assignments are based on the supplied JSON and subsequent requested updates. Month-only dates are marked TBC and shown after dated holidays in that month. Entries sharing a date but having different names remain separate. “All stores” and “All [state] Stores” refer to the stores present in the supplied data. This directory shows holiday assignments, not opening hours or closure rules.
